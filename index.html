How to play

Roll. Hold. Conquer.

Every turn is a quick gamble. Your dice decide whether you strike, heal, gather supplies, or march into the main keep.

01

Roll

Throw six dice up to three times. Tap any die to keep it and reroll the rest.

02

Resolve

Strikes hit rivals, Rally heals you, Supplies fuel your Stratagems, and Banners I–III score Glory.

03

Arm up

Spend Supplies in the Armory on Stratagems that bend the rules of the battle.

Your objective

Reach 20 Glory. Or stand last.

Hold the Main keep to earn a point just for being there. Wipe out your rivals, or outscore them. Either way, the castle is yours.

  • Win by reaching 20 Glory
  • Win by being the last commander standing
  • Lose when your health hits zero
